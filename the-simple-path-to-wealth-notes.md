## There's A Major Market Crash Coming!!!! And Even Famous Economists Can't Save You

Collins provides additional opinions about the market:
1. Market crashes are to be expected.
1. The market always recovers. In the event that it doesn't, none of this financial stuff will matter anyway.
1. The market always goes up.
1. The market is the single best-performing investment class over time.
1. The next ten, twenty, thirty, forty, fifty years will have just as many collapses, recessions, and disasters as in the past. Every time, it will be scary as hell. Every time, all the smart guys will be screaming, "Sell!!" And every time, only those few with enough nerve will stay the course and prosper.
1. This is why you have to toughen up, learn to ignore the noise, and ride out the storm, adding still more to your investments as you go.
1. To be strong enough to stay the course, you need to know these bad things are coming -- not only intellectually but on an emotional level as well.
1. There's a major market crash coming!! And there'll be another after that!! What wonderful buying opportunities they'll be.

## The Market Always Goes Up

Collins talks about how the market in the long-term has always trended up, despite notable downturns in the short term.

He champions Vanguard's Total Stock Market Index Fund, VTSAX, which holds shares of thousands of companies.

Collins reasons that the market always goes up because:
1. The market is self-cleansing.
1. Owning a stock is owning a part of living, breathing, dynamic companies, each striving to succeed.
