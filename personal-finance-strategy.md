### Consideration Of Risk

Investing in the market involves risk. But not investing in the market also involves risk.

### Where To Invest

In order of priority:
1. Fund 401(k)-type plans to the full employer match, if any.
1. Fully fund a Roth if your income is low enough that you are paying little or no income tax.
1. Once your income rate rises, fully fund a deductible IRA rather than the Roth.
1. Keep the Roth you started and just let it grow.
1. Finish funding the 401(k)-type plan to the max.
1. Consider funding a nondeductible IRA if your income is such that you cannot contribute to a deductible IRA or Roth IRA.
1. Fund your taxable account with any money left.

### What To Invest

Use ETFs at least for taxable accounts because in the event you wish to change brokers, ETFs like VTI can be transferred over in-kind as opposed to their mutual fund equivalents which would force you to sell potentially causing a taxable event.

### Recommended Funds

The following are low-cost index funds that track an array of stocks and bonds.

Stocks:
- VTI (Vanguard Total Stock Market Index Fund ETF)
- VXUS (Vanguard Total International Stock Index Fund ETF)

Bonds:
- BND (Vanguard Total Bond Market Index Fund ETF)
